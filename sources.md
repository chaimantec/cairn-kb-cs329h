# Sources — CS329H

Every course document this knowledge base draws on or points to, with its canonical
URL. Nothing here is committed to the repo as a binary: this knowledge base is read
by an agent that navigates markdown, so the URL is what is useful. Cite these URLs
directly.

**The course publishes no slide decks.** The Autumn 2024 site, its `slides/` path,
and the Autumn 2023 and Autumn 2025 archives were all checked on 2026-11-01, and checked
again before lectures 2, 3, 4, 5, 6 and 7 were added; none links a lecture deck. The course root now
carries an Autumn 2026 offering, checked before lectures 6 and 7 were added; it links no deck either. This knowledge base is therefore built from the lecture
transcripts, and the wiki cites transcript timestamps rather than slide numbers.

## Textbook

S. Truong and S. Koyejo, *Machine Learning from Human Preferences*, Stanford
University. Licensed CC BY-NC 4.0.

| Form | URL |
| --- | --- |
| Online book (current revision) | <https://mlhp.stanford.edu/> |
| PDF of the current revision | <https://mlhp.stanford.edu/Machine-Learning-from-Human-Preferences.pdf> |
| Source (Quarto) | <https://github.com/stair-lab/mlhp> |
| Link printed on the Autumn 2024 site | <https://ai.stanford.edu/~sttruong/mlhp/> (timed out on 2026-11-01) |

**The online book is not the text the 2024 lectures were taught from.** The 2024
lecture calls the book "a very rough cut" (transcript ≈2:24). The source repo's
history starts after that lecture, and the book was last revised in February 2026.
Its current chapters are Foundations, Learning, Action, Inversion and Aggregation,
which is not how the 2024 course was organised. This knowledge base does not take
content from the book. It was used only to check spellings while the transcripts
were copy-edited. A learner who wants a written treatment should go to the book
and expect its framing to differ from the lectures in places.
